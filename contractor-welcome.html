This Month's Focus — Cleaning Standards

As we move into September, please make sure the following are being done consistently on every account. These are the details clients notice first.

Floors
Mop thoroughly every single day
Not just spot-cleaned
Check corners and edges
No standing water or streaks
Vents & Baseboards
Dust vents on a regular rotation
Wipe down every baseboard
Don't skip low-visibility areas
Consistency is non-negotiable
